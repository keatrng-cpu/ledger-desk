**Scope:** Calibration, favorite–longshot bias (FLB), measurable signals, honest scoring. No trade recommendations.

## 1. Calibration & favorite–longshot bias

### Classic / peer-reviewed

- **Snowberg & Wolfers (2010, *JPE*)** — Horse-race FLB is better explained by **probability misperception** (prospect-theory style) than risk-love; longshots overbet, favorites underbet ([NBER w15923](https://www.nber.org/papers/w15923); [JPE](https://www.journals.uchicago.edu/doi/abs/10.1086/655844)).
- **Snowberg, Wolfers & Zitzewitz (2012, NBER w18222)** — Rule of thumb: treat contracts implying risk-neutral probs in **0–10% or 90–100%** with extreme caution; Intrade 2004 election contracts also show FLB-style under/over-prediction at the extremes ([PDF](https://www.nber.org/system/files/working_papers/w18222/w18222.pdf)).
- **Page & Clemen (2013, *Economic Journal*)** — Large Intrade-type sample (**1,787** markets): FLB **worsens with time to expiration**; markets are **reasonably well calibrated when time to expiry is short**, but biased farther out. Example nonparametric calibration: price **0.20 → ~15.3%** realized frequency; price **0.80 → ~87.4%** ([abstract](https://academic.oup.com/ej/article-abstract/123/568/491/5079498); QUT preprint summary).
- **Wolfers & Zitzewitz (2004, *JEP*)** — Foundational survey of prediction markets as forecasts ([JEP](https://www.aeaweb.org/articles?id=10.1257/0895330041371321)).
- **Rothschild (2009)** — Debiasing Intrade prices (incl. for FLB) improved election forecast accuracy vs polls early / in uncertain races ([Public Opinion Quarterly](https://academic.oup.com/poq/article-abstract/73/5/895/1835470) / [SSRN PDF](https://researchdmr.com/files/ForecastingElectionMarketsPolls.pdf)).

### Kalshi-specific (working paper — credible, not yet peer-reviewed journal)

**Bürgi, Deng & Whelan (2025, UCD WP25/19; also CEPR DP20631)** — First systematic Kalshi pricing study: **>300k** contract prices from **46,282** Yes contracts / **12,403** events (vol ≥ $1k; markets ≥24h; 2021–Apr 2025) ([UCD PDF](https://www.ucd.ie/economics/t4media/WP2025_19.pdf)).

| Finding (as stated by authors) | Evidence class |
|--------------------------------|----------------|
| Prices **informative**; **MAE falls** as market approaches close (steep drop on final day) | Documented (working paper) |
| Clear **FLB**: low-price contracts win less often than needed to break even; high-price win more | Documented |
| Contracts **≤10¢**: average loss rates **over 60%** (pre-fee pattern described; post-fee similar shape) | Documented |
| Average return **pre-fee ≈ −20%**, **post-fee ≈ −22%** (asymmetric: cheap-contract losses dominate) | Documented |
| **Makers** post-fee avg return **−11.99%**; **Takers −31.46%** (difference highly significant) | Documented |
| Makers on contracts **≥50¢**: ~**+2.6%** pre-fee / **+1.9%** post-fee (small but +) | Documented |
| Bias holds across categories, horizons to 10 days, volume/txn-size quintiles, years 2021–2025 (ψ weaker in 2025) | Documented |
| Fee formula used in paper: **$0.07 × p × (1−p)** per contract (round-up to cent); incidence hits **cheap** contracts harder as % of stake | Documented + matches Kalshi fee design |

Model fit (authors’ calibration): modest Maker optimism **δ ≈ 0.005**, required return **γ ≈ 0.04**, fee **θ = 0.07** reproduces Maker/Taker FLB shapes. Presentation slides also cite belief-weighting **β = 0.09** in a related calibration ([Whelan NYU slides](https://www.karlwhelan.com/Presentations/Whelan-NYU.pdf)).

### Polymarket (published platform stats + recent arXiv)

- **Polymarket accuracy page (platform, not peer-reviewed):** snapshots 1m / 1w / 1d / 12h / 4h before resolution — “accuracy” (leading side correct) **90.2% → 98.6%**; headline Brier **0.0627** ([polymarket.com/accuracy](https://polymarket.com/accuracy)). **Caveat:** near-resolution accuracy is partly mechanical (prices → 0/1); prefer event-anchored truncations ([Bellwether](https://bellwethermetrics.com/papers/election-eve-methodology/)).
- **Cardozo & Rivero-Wildemauwe (arXiv:2609.12878, Sep 2026):** 588M trades. Dollar-weighted: purchases **<10¢ lose 19.3¢/$**; **≥90¢ earn 0.83¢/$**. Equal-child-market: longshots **−6.3%**; equal-parent-event: longshots **+4.1%**. FLB **robust in Crypto & Politics**, **absent in Sports** ([arXiv](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.12878)).

## 2. Simple signals — documented vs anecdotal

| Signal | What sources say | Class |
|--------|------------------|-------|
| **Horizon / late accuracy** | Kalshi MAE declines toward close (Bürgi et al.); Polymarket accuracy rises toward resolution (platform page); Page & Clemen: short-horizon better calibrated | **Documented** (but near-expiry accuracy ≠ tradable edge without careful timing) |
| **FLB / longshot fade, favorite lean** | Kalshi ≤10¢ large losses; ≥50–70¢ small + post-fee for some bands (Bürgi et al.); Polymarket tails depend on aggregation (Cardozo et al.); sports betting FLB classic (Snowberg & Wolfers) | **Documented pattern**; **tradable edge after fees/spreads = open** |
| **Maker vs Taker** | Kalshi: Makers −12% vs Takers −31% post-fee; cheap contracts disproportionately taken (Bürgi et al.) | **Documented** microstructure |
| **Liquidity / volume** | Kalshi: FLB **not** eliminated in high-volume or large-txn quintiles (Bürgi et al. Tables 6–7). Polymarket thesis: volume–Brier link weak / unstable (Skemman thesis — treat as tentative) | **Documented that volume ≠ free lunch**; spread effects modeled but not a clean “wide-spread edge” result |
| **Bid–ask / fees** | Kalshi best Yes+No offers sum **>$1**; fee **round_up(M × 0.07 × C × P × (1−P))** for standard taker (PDF schedule / paper). Cheap side bears fee as larger % of capital | **Documented** — any signal must be **fee+slippage** tested |
| **News-reaction lag** | Literature notes continuous incorporation (Snowberg et al. handbook); Linnaimaa-style limit-order risk on news cited by Bürgi et al. as open for Kalshi | **Anecdotal / open** for Kalshi—no systematic lag-edge paper in sources above |
| **Yogi Berra / late sports bias** | Page (2012): late underdog sports prices too high on Intrade | **Documented** (Intrade sports); Kalshi sports FLB weaker in Bürgi category splits |

**Fee survival:** With Kalshi-style fees peaking near *p*=0.5 and high *as % of stake* near extremes, raw FLB on longshots is **after-fee worse**. Small favorite-side Maker returns (~1.9% post-fee on ≥50¢ in Bürgi et al.) may **not** survive spread + capacity + risk (authors discuss why bias isn’t competed away: small volumes, high return SD ~33% on Maker ≥50¢, information lag).

## 3. How to score a signal honestly

| Tool | Role | Cite |
|------|------|------|
| **Brier score** | Mean $(f-o)^2$; lower better | Brier (1950); Polymarket docs |
| **Murphy decomposition** | **B = REL − RES + UNC** (reliability − resolution + uncertainty) | Murphy (1973); survey [arXiv:1303.6182](https://arxiv.org/pdf/1303.6182) |
| **Log loss** | Sharper penalty for confident wrong calls | Standard probabilistic scoring |
| **Reliability diagram / calibration curve** | Bin forecast *p* vs realized frequency (Page & Clemen Fig. 4 style; Bürgi Fig. 3) | Page & Clemen 2013; Bürgi et al. |
| **Mincer–Zarnowitz** | Regress $y-p$ on $p$; test $\alpha=\psi=0$ | Used throughout Bürgi et al. |
| **Sample size / CIs** | Event-**and** contract-clustered SEs (Bürgi); parent-event clustering (Cardozo); bootstrap for bin frequencies | Same papers |
| **Multiple testing / snooping** | Pre-register price bins, domains, Maker/Taker; hold out years (Bürgi finds weaker ψ in 2025) | Standard; anomaly fade literature cited by Bürgi §6 |
| **OOS / walk-forward** | Train on past resolutions; score only **pre-outcome** snapshots (Bellwether warning on resolution-relative prices) | Bellwether methodology |
| **Fee/slippage P&L** | Use Kalshi fee formula + mid→touch; report Maker vs Taker separately | Kalshi fee schedule; Bürgi eqs. (2)–(3) |

## 4. What to build first (minimal engine)

1. **Price-bin FLB monitor** — Win rate & post-fee return by 10¢ bins, split Maker/Taker and category; Mincer–Zarnowitz F-tests with event clustering. *Source: Bürgi et al.*
2. **Horizon MAE / Brier curves** — Snapshot prices at T−10d … T−0 (and fixed clock offsets); track MAE & Brier vs resolution; **truncate before known outcome**. *Sources: Bürgi Fig. 4; Polymarket accuracy methods; Bellwether.*
3. **Calibration / reliability diagram + Brier REL/RES/UNC** — Platform baseline vs any “signal-adjusted” probability. *Sources: Murphy; Page & Clemen.*
4. **Fee-adjusted expectancy calculator** — Implement `fees ≈ round_up(0.07 × C × P × (1−P))` (and series multipliers from Kalshi schedule); refuse signals that don’t clear fee+1¢ spread. *Sources: Bürgi; [kalshi.com/fee-schedule](https://kalshi.com/fee-schedule) / fee PDF.*
5. **Walk-forward harness** — Rolling year holdouts; report CI on Brier Δ and on post-fee mean return; no look-ahead on resolution lag. *Sources: Bürgi year splits; Cardozo aggregation sensitivity.*

**Do not build first:** discretionary “news lag” scalps or un-fee’d longshot buying — pattern is **documented as a loss**, not an edge (Bürgi; Cardozo dollar-weighted longshots).
