## Method
- Sample: 23,293 5m RTH displacements ES+NQ 2010-07…2026-10-14 (deep-audit v3 `feat.parquet`), target R (2R/stop/time, futures, gross).
- **Walk-forward only** (`wf.py`): for each test year 2014…2026, ridge (α=50) on all prior years; a factor gets a nonzero weight only if its 90% bootstrap CI on train excludes 0. q-weight = 0.2 × β(R), clamped ±0.06. Last window (train 2010–2025) gives the shipped weights. OOS = 2014-01…2026-10 (668 weeks).
- `ev.py` → `eval.json`: per-factor OOS R lift with 90% bootstrap CI, split by session (open30 08:30–09:00, am 09–11, mid, pm) and day type; calibration by band; fires/week; PF.
- **Caveat:** the 16-year set is a mechanical proxy. The live q layers (template modelQ, iFVG kills, plan penalties) can't be rebuilt on history, so live q there is a proxy (`q_live` in wf.py). Gross futures R, not option P&L (optimize-1015 shows option costs flip a ~+0.05R edge negative).

**Why each outranks the next:**
- The chase dominates because it multiplies the whole q (one 1×ATR chase halves q); everything else adds ≤0.03.
- The key sweep is the only Holm survivor (deep-audit v3) and is #1 in OOS lift. Untracked raises move q more often but can't be judged.
- The LTF kill moves more q than trend day but is a veto.
- Trend day beats SMT/PD on |lift| × n and has a positive sign; SMT/PD are smaller and negative. MSS is positive but weaker than trend day and absent the open edge. Vol is small; iFVG/HTF are indistinguishable from 0.

**Teaching vs data (honest):**
- ICT 2022 model / TJR / PB all teach **sweep → displacement/MSS → FVG/iFVG entry inside a killzone**.
  - DT Academy ICT model rules: https://www.thedtterminal.com/academy/ict-building-your-model
  - TJR Boot Camp Day 30, "liquidity sweep + break of structure … + FVG/OB + equilibrium": https://pickscribe.com/v/ESsy0uoFAz4/
  - Telonics TJR breakdown (09:30–09:50 ET sweep, 5m BOS/iFVG confirm, SMT gate, no trades after 10:30): https://telonicstrading.com/research-tjr.html
  - PB/IFVG model, SMT "a bonus, not a requirement": https://www.tradezella.com/strategies/ifvg-trading-model, https://dhesitrades.com/education/v4-inversion-model
  - X: the sweep → displacement → MSS → FVG checklist (https://x.com/AhmadICT01/status/2102508702137721152); sweep + SMT + iFVG recaps (https://x.com/traderxkira/status/2104981839920181685, https://x.com/VickyVisva99475/status/2100228286026748243). One of these is a −1R "sweep + SMT + iFVG + CISD" loss on 10/15 (https://x.com/miansunnyyyy/status/2110744405854560645).
- **The data agrees** on the first two links and on time: the key-level sweep, inside the killzone, is the one measurable edge.
- **The data disagrees on:**
  - **SMT** (taught as a confluence/gate; measured negative);
  - **premium/discount** (taught as "buy discount"; negative, because continuation entries after displacement sit in "premium" by construction, per strictness-1015);
  - **HTF bias** (taught first; ≈0 here);
  - **iFVG** (PB's trigger; ≈0 as a *score*, though it may still matter as a *trigger/kill*, which q_v2 keeps).
- PB explicitly downplays prior-session H/L sweeps (PB Theory video). Our data says those are exactly what pays.

**Proposed score sequence (matches the desk's pipeline: scanner → discipline → caps → seal):**
1. Base setup fit (sweep + displacement/MSS template, re-weighted).
2. Key-level sweep × time.
3. Regime (trend day, vol).
4. Live caps/kills (vetoes stay vetoes).
5. Option quality.
6. Seal at raise-only edges.

Timing (chase) is outside q, in entryState and the gates. q_v2 applies the factors in exactly this order, each logged and capped.

## Per-factor detail
Format: **mechanism / source / unseen-data effect / weight & rank / overlap**. OOS lifts are from `eval.json` (2014–2026 OOS).

**1. Chase (R3).**
- Mechanism: distance past the entry CE lowers R:R and fill quality. That is a timing property, not setup quality.
- Source: ICT/TJR "enter on the retrace to the FVG/CE, don't chase". This is an *entry rule*, not a grade.
- Effect: it's not a historical feature here. On the 10/15 cards it caused 50% of rows to lose −0.56 mean q.
- Weight: 0 in q_v2. The live gates (watcher entry_distance 0.5×ATR, loss.late, lateEntry.pnlMinPct) still refuse late fills.
- Overlap: it fed the seal clamp that was mislabelled "shadow:timing".

**2. Key sweep × time.**
- Mechanism: stops resting at ON/PD extremes are the largest pools. A displacement right after they are taken, at the open when the opening flows run, marks a completed raid.
- Source: ICT killzone + liquidity pools (PDH/PDL, Asia/London H/L); TJR session-high/low sweeps 09:30–10:30 ET.
- Effect: +0.225R [0.16, 0.28], n=1,632. By session: open30 +0.28, am +0.13, mid −0.17 (so the ramp ends at 11:00). By day type: range +0.26, trend +0.23. The key × trend-day interaction is +0.28 [0.17, 0.38], n=413, which contradicts deep-audit v3's "doesn't pay on trend days" (a different trend proxy and window).
- Weight: +0.06, fitted 0.052–0.059 across the last 3 windows. Rank 2.
- Overlap: sweep_significant (4/104 in structure) is kept. Mild double-count, < 0.01.

**3. Untracked raises.**
- Mechanism: logging defect. DISP/REVERSAL nudges were written on card copies outside the tracked setter.
- Source: deep-audit v3 B.
- Effect: +0.11 mean on 94% of rows. Not predictive-testable.
- Handling: a residual entry, so every delta has a source. q_v2 carries these deltas, capped at ±0.07.

**4. LTF reversal kill.**
- Mechanism: a 5m iFVG / 1m wick against the side invalidates the setup.
- Source: PB inversion logic, ICT "failed FVG".
- Effect: replay-only evidence (fire-fail-1015). iFVG as a score is ≈0.
- Handling: a veto (q_v2 band C when vetoed), not a weight. Overlap with iFVG weight is why that weight is cut.

**5. Trend day.**
- Mechanism: when price is far from the open relative to the day's range, displacement in an established one-way day carries further.
- Source: ICT daily profiles (trend vs consolidation days), TJR "continuation".
- Effect: +0.107 [0.07, 0.14], n=8,325. By session: open30 +0.17, mid +0.09, am 0.00, pm +0.05.
- Weight: +0.02 (fitted 0.019 in every recent window). Rank 5.
- Overlap: interacts positively with the key sweep (above). Negatively with MSS on trend days (MSS −0.03 there).

**6. SMT.**
- Mechanism (as taught): ES/NQ divergence at a sweep shows one index refused the move.
- Source: ICT SMT, PB/TJR gate, many X recaps.
- Effect: −0.082 [−0.14, −0.01], n=1,406. Negative in every session (mid −0.18). The WF fit wanted −0.02.
- Weight: **0** (spec), and the SMT band cap is not carried into q_v2. The data argues for a small negative weight; we chose 0 to avoid fitting a penalty to a detector proxy.

**7. Premium/discount.**
- Mechanism (as taught): buy below equilibrium, sell above.
- Effect: −0.064 [−0.10, −0.03], n=9,412. Open30 −0.12.
- Weight: 0. Overlap: P/D × open window is the top negative SHAP interaction.

**8. MSS.**
- Mechanism: a close through the last opposing swing confirms the order flow changed.
- Source: core of all three schools.
- Effect: +0.066 [0.03, 0.10], n=12,260. am +0.05, mid +0.12, pm −0.03; trend days −0.03.
- Weight: 4 (unchanged). It ranks below trend day because its lift is smaller and it disappears on trend days.

**9. Volatility.**
- Mechanism: high prior-day range means wider noise around the same ATR-scaled stop.
- Effect: −0.060 [−0.09, −0.03], n=12,923. Open30 −0.13, am +0.05.
- Weight: −0.01 (fitted −0.007). The live desk has no 250-day range percentile yet, so `volPct` is null and the term is inactive until wired. Stated plainly.

**10. iFVG.**
- Mechanism: a gap traded through flips its role, which shows order-flow change.
- Source: PB trading's core trigger.
- Effect: −0.015 [−0.05, +0.02], n=6,783. ≈0 in every session.
- Weight: 3 per the requested change. **It fails the "justify a nonzero weight" test.** Recommendation: 0 as a score; keep it as the kill/trigger.

**11. HTF.**
- Effect: −0.001 [−0.03, +0.03], n=8,063.
- Weight: halved per spec (htf2 2, weekly_pd 2, mid_bias 4, daily 1.5, bonus 0.0125). **It also fails the nonzero test.** Recommendation: 0 as a score; keep R4 as the band cap.

**12. OB / FVG tap.**
- On 91–92% of rows, so they carry almost no information. OB's OOS +0.088 is the 8% *without* an OB doing badly.
- Weight: 0.

**13. Option quality (new, Keaton-approved).**
- Mechanism: the trade is expressed in 0DTE/1DTE SPY/QQQ options, and optimize-1015 showed the spread + theta turn +0.046R into −$8/trade. Three penalties, total capped at 0.07:
  - **spread % > 3%:** −0.5×(excess), max −0.04;
  - **premium / expected move > 1.0:** −0.5×(excess), max −0.03;
  - **0DTE from 13:00 CT:** −0.01 per 30 minutes, max −0.03.
- Source: the desk's own fill (2.97/2.98) and optimize-1015; standard 0DTE theta decay into the close.
- Effect: **no historical chain data** (free Databento has no OPRA here), so these weights are priors, not fits. They are inactive (`option: null`) until the ticket's live quote is wired in. Rank: last; it applies after setup quality.

**14. Per-factor cap (new).**
- Every factor delta is clamped to ±0.07 (`factorCap`), the widest band (A, 0.68–0.75). So no single input can move q more than one grade band. Live, the chase alone moves it 0.56 (C↔A+).
- Kills remain vetoes, outside the cap, by design.

**15. Drift monitor (new).**
- `python3 scripts/qv2_drift.py cards.jsonl --out drift.md` compares realized win rate (Wilson 95%) and mean R (normal 95%, OOS σ) per q_v2 band with `shared/q-v2-expect.json`. It exits 1 and flags ⚠ when the WF expectation is outside the realized CI.
- Not scheduled. It needs a daily cards.jsonl with outcomes, and wiring a cron is a separate, explicit step.

**16. Keaton's calls (held-out acceptance; never fitted).**
- `accept.py` → `accept_keaton.csv`. 5 calls 10/14–10/15 (strictness-1015 reconstruction): 4 winners.
  - **Live q:** "fires" (≥ B+) 2/5, hit rate **3/5 (60%)**.
  - **q_v2:** a conservative lower bound (qraw + key + regime). It fires 4/5 and its hit rate is also **3/5 (60%)**: it catches the 10/14 08:50 short (+2.2R) but also rates the 10/15 08:54 chase short (−1R) at B+ or better. That one is the entry gate's job, and the gate refused it.
- The 08:55 flip long has no card in either.
- n=5, so this proves nothing either way.

- **The gain is selection, not more trades.** q_v2's calibration is clean and monotone, so A/A+ under q_v2 mean something (+0.16/+0.31R). At the live volume it is no better.
- **After option costs nothing here is shown profitable** (optimize-1015). Treat q_v2 as a better *ranking*, not an edge.
- **10/13–10/14 replay** (mechanical, Databento): 13 detections.
  - Live fires 13, +5.3R total, **+0.41R/trade**.
  - q_v2 fires 9, +1.3R, **+0.15R/trade**.
  - q_v2 drops the 10/14 08:50/08:55 ES+NQ shorts (+2R each; q_v2 0.588, no key sweep). **q_v2 was worse on these two days.**
- **10/15 replay** (4,480 cards.log rows; `replay1015.txt`): removing the chase lifts the ES/MNQ *long* cards from C to B+…A+ from 12:31 CT (2,507 rows ≥ B+ vs 304 live). Those cards were 1×ATR+ late, so the entry_distance gate would still refuse them. **Fires are unchanged.** Shorts are unchanged.
- Paper trades: not re-scored (no per-trade q_v2 inputs were logged). The drift monitor is the path to that.
